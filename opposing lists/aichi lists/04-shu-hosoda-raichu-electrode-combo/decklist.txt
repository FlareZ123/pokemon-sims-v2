Usage:
This is Shu Hosoda's top-4 CL Aichi 2026 Electro Rain OTK list. It is a combo deck whose intended game is radically shorter than a normal Prize race. The primary plan is to create a protected setup window with Gengar & Mimikyu-GX's Horror House-GX, then deliberately give up four Prizes through Electrode-GX and use Alolan Raichu's Electro Rain to take all six opposing Prizes in one attack.

The first setup task is to assemble Horror House-GX when the opponent would otherwise interact before the combo. Unit Energy LPM can satisfy Psychic for Gengar & Mimikyu-GX. Horror House-GX prevents the opponent from playing cards from hand on their following turn, effectively creating the extra development window the OTK needs. The brain should not confuse this with a conventional control plan: Horror House is usually protecting the combo clock.

Battle Compressor is central because Electrode-GX's Extra Energy Bomb attaches up to five Energy cards from the discard pile to a non-GX/non-EX Pokémon and then Knocks Out Electrode-GX. The deck wants Special Energy in the discard, Voltorb/Electrode established, and Alolan Raichu ready to receive those Energy. Two Extra Energy Bomb uses give the opponent four Prizes while loading a large Energy stack onto Alolan Raichu. The Prize deficit also makes Counter Energy/Reversal Energy operate in their intended comeback mode. Electro Rain then discards Lightning-providing Energy from Alolan Raichu and assigns repeated 30-damage packets across the opposing board until six Prizes can be taken.

Quick Ball, Dedenne-GX, Crobat V, Squawkabilly ex, Tapu Lele-GX, Eldegoss V, Sky Field and Forest Seal Stone form a high-output draw/search engine. The policy should burn these aggressively when doing so completes the OTK because unused long-game resources have almost no value if the combo wins immediately. Conversely, Bench capacity and sequencing are critical: support Pokémon consume space needed for Voltorb/Electrode and Raichu, so Sky Field replacement or Sudowoodo-style Bench limits must be respected.

Rescue Stretcher is unusually important because the combo repeatedly needs Pokémon pieces after self-Knock-Outs and discard-heavy setup. Escape Rope is both mobility and a way to alter opposing targets. Telescopic Sight is a damage modifier for appropriate Benched multi-Prize targets and should be evaluated against the exact board rather than assumed mandatory.

The deck is fragile to disruption. Ability lock can stop Extra Energy Bomb or the draw engine; Item lock can disconnect Battle Compressor/search/recovery; Bench Barrier can invalidate the spread kill; attack-cost taxes such as Active Goomy can stop or delay Horror House-GX/Electro Rain; Wobbuffet can suppress most of the Rule Box setup engine. If the main OTK route is disconnected, preserve any credible fallback rather than sacrificing four Prizes blindly.

Primary objective: complete Horror House -> double Electrode-GX acceleration -> six-Prize Electro Rain as early as legally possible, with all sequencing chosen around protecting that exact combo.
